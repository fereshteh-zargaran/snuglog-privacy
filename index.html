Snuglog

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Snuglog is an offline-first baby care, growth, and developmental companion that helps parents log feeding sessions, sleep, diaper changes, medicine, growth measurements, developmental milestones, and memory journal entries. Your privacy is a priority — this policy explains exactly how the app handles your information.

Information You Enter

Snuglog stores only the information you choose to enter on your device, including:

  • Baby profile details (name, gender, and date of birth)
  • Feeding logs, side alternation, and session durations
  • Sleep and nap records
  • Diaper change and medicine dosage records
  • Growth measurements (weight, length/height, head circumference, and checkup notes)
  • Developmental milestone progress
  • Memory Journal notes, moods, and optional photos you explicitly select via Apple's PhotosPicker
  • Reminder and unit preferences

How Your Data Is Stored

All data is stored entirely on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework and local storage (UserDefaults). Snuglog requires no account, makes zero network calls, and never transmits your data to any external server.

🔒 Data shared between Snuglog and its Home Screen, Lock Screen, and Control Center widgets is kept strictly inside a local App Group container on your device only.

Photos, Audio & PDF Exports

  • Photos: Optional memory photos are selected using Apple's privacy-preserving PhotosPicker and stored locally on your device. Snuglog does not request access to your full photo library or camera.
  • Soothing Audio: Built-in sleep sounds (pink noise, rain, heartbeat, white noise, and lullaby chimes) are synthesized locally offline on your device. Snuglog never uses the microphone or records audio.
  • PDF & Summary Sharing: Pediatrician Visit Summary PDFs and Daily Summary cards are generated locally on your device and are only shared when you explicitly choose an app or recipient via the iOS Share Sheet.

Notifications

Snuglog uses Apple's local notification system to send optional feeding reminders. These reminders are scheduled and delivered entirely on-device, and no notification content is ever sent to external servers.

Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

Snuglog does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash reporting SDKs. The app uses only Apple system frameworks and does not collect, share, or sell any personal data.

Medical & Reference Information Disclaimer

Growth charts and milestone checklists are provided for personal informational tracking only and are adapted from WHO Child Growth Standards and CDC Developmental Milestones. They are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult your pediatrician.

Children's Privacy

Snuglog is designed for use by parents and caregivers. Because all data stays exclusively on the parent's device, Snuglog does not knowingly collect personal data from children or parents.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
